// about

Systems, end to end.

I’m a Real LIFE Foundation scholar graduate, and it’s what I lead with, because it came with the principle I still operate by: Leadership, Integrity, Faith, Excellence. That’s the standard I hold in every room I end up in and every piece of work I take, critical or routine. Going the extra mile is the default, not the exception.

I graduated Magna Cum Laude in Computer Science from the Polytechnic University of the Philippines in Manila, backed by merit scholarships from DOST-SEI, the Real LIFE Foundation, Taguig City’s LANI program and the NEON Foundation. I lead in the communities I join — founding member of the first AWS Cloud Club in the Philippines, recognized by AWS itself, and programming lead at my university’s Cisco Network Organization.

Full-stack engineer since 2022 — and the range is the point: marketing sites at one end; AI analytics, clinical operations and multi-branch enterprise platforms at the other. I’ve trained my own models, built a programming language from scratch, shipped Unity games and owned releases through CI/CD. AWS is where I’m formally credentialed; PHP/Laravel, Python and JavaScript are daily tools, never a ceiling. Some of it sits here, the rest behind NDA.
